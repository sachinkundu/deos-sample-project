## Why

People need a clear way to report problems with the sample app. A short guide will help them share useful facts and keep private data safe.

## What Changes

- Add a short guide for reporting a problem with the sample app.
- Ask readers what happened, what they expected, and how to repeat the problem.
- Tell readers not to include passwords or private data in the report.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `sample-app-problem-reporting`: Guide people through a clear and safe problem report.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This adds user guide text for the sample app. It does not change app behavior, APIs, or dependencies.
